# **Rules and Guidelines**

## **1\. Project Scope**

* The project must address a **real-world or professionally relevant engineering problem**, not a trivial or textbook exercise. (PI2.4.1)

* The problem must be **complex enough to require creativity, abstraction, and integration** across at least two sub-disciplines of computer engineering. (PI2.4.1, PI3.5.1)

* Both **design (Semester 1\)** and **implementation (Semester 2\)** are mandatory components. (PI3.5.1, PI3.5.2)

## **2\. Teamwork**

* Each project must be carried out by a **team of 3–5 students**. (PI6.9.1, PI6.9.2)

* Teams must practice **effective coordination, division of roles, and project management** throughout both semesters. (PI6.9.1, PI6.9.2)

* Individual contributions must be documented via **weekly progress updates**. (PI6.9.2)

## **3\. Supervision**

* Each team will have **advisors** from the department and project owner and may have **co-advisors** (from industry, academia, or research institutes).

* Students are responsible for **maintaining regular communication** with their advisor(s). (PI6.9.2)

* Advisors provide **guidance**, but students are expected to **drive the project independently**.

## **4\. Deliverables & Timeline**

* **Semester 1:** Proposal Stage

  * Written proposal (English or Thai) – evaluated for problem definition, problem solving, design, teamwork, ethics, and communication.

  * Proposal presentation (Thai) – evaluated for speaking and clarity of technical ideas.

  * ~~Ethics seminar and quiz – supports evaluation of ethical knowledge.~~

* **Semester 2:** Implementation & Final Stage

  * Final written report (English or Thai) – evaluated for implementation, testing/experiments, analysis of results, environment/society, and professional standards.

  * Final oral presentation (Thai) – evaluated for speaking, teamwork, and ability to answer questions.

* Reports may be **revised based on committee feedback** to maximize quality.

## **5\. Evaluation Rules**

* Evaluations are based primarily on the **content of the written report and slides**.

* Presentations are used for **clarification**, but scores are determined by documented content.

* Each outcome is evaluated using a **0–4 scale** (Excellent → Poor), aligned with **TABEE PIs**.

* Projects will be evaluated by **multiple examiners**, not only the advisor.

* To progress from Semester 1 → Semester 2, projects must **pass the proposal evaluation**.

## **6\. Ethics & Professional Conduct**

* Students must demonstrate **academic honesty** — plagiarism or falsified data will result in failure. (PI9.9.1, PI9.9.2)

* Respect for **intellectual property, fairness in teamwork, and professional behavior** is required. (PI9.9.2)

* Ethical awareness and practice will be assessed through both **seminar/quiz (subject to change)**  and **application in the project**. (PI9.9.1, PI9.9.2)

## **7\. Management & Constraints**

* Teams must show awareness of **risks, costs, and resource limitations**. (PI10.0.1, PI10.0.2)

* **Environmental impacts** (e.g., resource efficiency, sustainability) must be considered. (PI8.5.1)

* **Societal and professional responsibilities** (e.g., safety, inclusivity, public benefit) must be addressed. (PI8.7.1)

**Instructions for Using the Proposal Template**

**1\. Structure**

* Follow the **template sections in order** (Basic Information → Abstract → Introduction → Problem Solving → Design → Teamwork → Timeline → Ethics → References).

* Include a **Table of Contents** with correct page numbers.

* Ensure **page numbering** is continuous throughout the document.

* Use **clear section headings and subheadings** so evaluators can easily locate information.

**2\. English or Thai**

* Write in **formal academic English or Thai** that is clear, concise, and grammatically correct.

* Avoid casual language, bullet-only descriptions, or incomplete sentences.

* Use consistent tense and style throughout the report.

**3\. Figures and Diagrams**

* All diagrams must be **accurate, clear, and directly related** to the content.

* Each figure or diagram must have a **number, title, and caption** (e.g., *Figure 1: System Architecture*).

* Refer to all figures in the main text (e.g., *as shown in Figure 1*).

* Ensure diagrams are **readable when printed** (labels not too small).

**4\. References**

* Use a **consistent referencing style** (IEEE or APA recommended).

* Every in-text citation must have a corresponding entry in the References section.

* References must include **complete details** (authors, title, publication, year, etc.).

* Reference format must be **consistent throughout** the document.

**5\. Citation and Academic Honesty**

* Cite **all sources properly**, including figures, diagrams, data, and text.

* Direct quotations must be placed in quotation marks and cited.

* Use plagiarism-check tools (e.g., Turnitin via Capstone account) before submission.

* Failure to cite properly or submitting plagiarized work will result in rejection of the proposal.
